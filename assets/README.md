# Maps and Reference Imagery

Working reference maps for *Labile Advection*. Geography, contamination zones, and movement routes across the three POV regions. All images are generated reference, not finished art.

## Ireland

- **`ireland--fallout_map.png`** — Radioactive contamination zones across the island: the permanent Sellafield plutonium exclusion covering Northern Ireland, the cesium-137 belt across Waterford–Limerick from Hinkley Point, the Irish Sea dead zone, and the clean agricultural south.
- **`ireland--french_occupation_map.png`** — Distribution of French military and administrative presence across southern Ireland as authority migrates from a collapsing continent.
- **`ireland--northern_refugee_routes_map.png`** — Principal routes by which refugees moved south out of the Northern Ireland exclusion zone after Sellafield and Liverpool fell.

## China / Southeast Asia

- **`china--collective_network_map.png`** — The network of surviving collectives across the Chinese interior, with Wuyishan highlighted. Terrain-shielded valleys where working pre-collapse technology persists.
- **`china--fujian_contamination_map.png`** — The mutation zone in Fujian produced by the H-bomb and chemical contamination events of the escalation wars.
- **`china--journey_map.png`** — Tian Hongpao's journey path across China, from Shanghai in the collapse years to the Wuyishan collective.
- **`china--tungapuri_border_map.png`** — Eastern Shan State / Kengtung border region (Tungapuri), where her younger sibling has resettled.

## France

- **`france--fallout_map.png`** — Continental-scale contamination from France's 56 reactor sites and the La Hague reprocessing plant, with plumes into the Mediterranean, Atlantic, Channel, and Rhône outflow.
