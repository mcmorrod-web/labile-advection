# World Timeline

## Prequel Era — The Road to Collapse

The prequel hints at the origin of the war without fully explaining the chain. The starting point is recognisable: the current Iran conflict.

### Escalation Sequence (draft)

- The Iran war begins as a regional conflict — recognisable to a present-day reader
- Escalation draws in major powers; alliances fracture and reform unpredictably
- Economic sanctions, energy disruption, and supply chain collapse spread instability far beyond the Middle East
- Proxy conflicts multiply across Central Asia, the South China Sea, and East Africa
- A threshold is crossed — nuclear or chemical weapons are used (H-bomb reference in collective backstory)
- **High-altitude EMP (HEMP) strikes** accompany the nuclear exchange — standard doctrine to disable communications and electronics across entire regions. A single 1.4 MT burst at 400km altitude covers a radius of 1,000-1,500km. Key events:
  - **HEMP over the UK / North Atlantic**: Covers all of Britain and all of Ireland. Every unshielded electronic device on the island of Ireland is fried — even though no bombs fall on Irish soil. Neural implants in anyone outdoors cause seizures and brain damage from induced current. Ireland's low mountains (~1,039m) offer no meaningful terrain shadow.
  - **HEMP over eastern China**: Covers Fujian, Jiangxi, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Hunan and beyond. Coastal and lowland electronics are destroyed. Mountain-shielded valleys (e.g. Wuyishan, behind 2,000m peaks) see EMP field reduced by ~90-95%, allowing many devices to survive — this is why the Wuyishan collective retains more working technology than lowland communities. The AI may have steered early collective formation toward terrain-shielded locations for this reason. See `notes/china--collective_network.md`.
  - These HEMP events are why **functioning neural implants are rare at 12 years** — most implanted people in unshielded locations were killed or brain-damaged by their own hardware during the initial pulses. Surviving implants exist mainly in mountain-shielded zones and among those who were underground or inside metal-framed buildings at the moment of detonation. See `notes/china--implant_technology.md`.
- Global economic systems collapse in a cascade, not a single event
- China's coastal cities (including Shanghai) experience economic implosion, infrastructure failure, and mass displacement
- The collapse is not a single war but an overlapping series of wars, economic failures, and environmental disasters that compound each other
- The UK is severely affected — multiple nuclear sites compromised:
  - **Sellafield** (Cumbria) hit or breached: Releases ~140 tonnes of stored plutonium, decades of spent fuel and legacy waste. The Irish Sea becomes contaminated. Plutonium-239 (half-life 24,000 years) makes Northern Ireland a permanent exclusion zone.
  - **Hinkley Point** (Somerset) hit: Reactor-level contamination enters the Bristol Channel. Under northerly/northeasterly winds, fallout crosses the Celtic Sea and deposits along a band through Waterford and Limerick.
  - Liverpool also suffers direct strike or catastrophic industrial contamination, adding to Northern Ireland's fallout load
- Ireland is split by contamination into distinct zones (see `notes/ireland--fallout_research.md`):
  - **Northern Ireland**: Permanent exclusion zone (Sellafield plutonium + Liverpool fallout). Belfast, Derry abandoned. Not coming back.
  - **East coast / Dublin**: Compromised from Irish Sea contamination and easterly wind fallout events. Dublin depopulated. Two late pre-collapse megaprojects — the **Three Rock** vertical city in the Dublin Mountains and **Poolbeg Island** in Dublin Bay — sit empty in the contamination zone and are the highest-value (and most dangerous) salvage targets on the island. See `notes/ireland--dublin.md`.
  - **Waterford-Limerick band**: Cesium-137 contamination belt from Hinkley Point. Not farmable at 12 years; partial recovery in 30-50 years.
  - **South of the line**: Clean. Agricultural, mild, functional. One of the better places on Earth post-collapse.
  - **Irish Sea**: Dead water. No fishing. Contaminated from Sellafield waste.
- **France**: All 56 reactors across 18+ sites hit, plus La Hague reprocessing plant (half the world's spent fuel capacity). France is essentially uninhabitable. Rivers carry contamination to the Mediterranean, Atlantic, and Channel. See `notes/ireland--france_europe_fallout_research.md`
- Western Europe is devastated: Germany, Belgium, Luxembourg, Netherlands all contaminated from French fallout plus own sites
- The Mediterranean becomes a contaminated semi-enclosed sea (Rhône outflow from 4 reactor sites)
- Surviving pockets in Europe: Southern Ireland, deep Pyrenees, Portugal, possibly Corsica, Switzerland (underground)
- **Neural implant technology** was widespread pre-collapse: consumer-grade (pet monitoring, lifestyle), professional-grade (military, technical workers), and research-grade (lab animals, human test subjects). China was a major centre for this research. When the collapse hit, implanted individuals and animals survived with their hardware intact — the AI network that later controlled the collectives inherited access to these devices. See `notes/china--implant_technology.md`
- **A lunar base** existed before the collapse — a permanent scientific installation (plausible near-future given Artemis programme, Chinese lunar ambitions). When Earth collapsed, the base was cut off but survived on closed-loop life support. At 12 years: a skeleton crew of dozens, ageing, finite, observing Earth from 384,000km. A detective on the base is piecing together what happened below from fragmentary data — telescopic observation, degraded satellite relay, intercepted radio/network signals. See `characters/moon--detective.md`
- By the time the dust settles, no single power controls anything; the world reorganises locally

### Prequel Tone and Approach

- The Iran war should be hinted at, not explained — a news broadcast, a conversation fragment, a date on a document
- The reader should recognise it the way you recognise a familiar street in a photograph of a bombed city
- The escalation should feel inevitable in retrospect but chaotic in the moment
- Avoid making any single nation "the villain" — the collapse is systemic, not conspiratorial
- The prequel can show Tian Hongpao's Shanghai life in the final years before collapse: the economy tightening, the news getting worse, the family fracturing under pressure

### Connection to Main Novel

- Tian Hongpao was in her late teens when the collapse hit Shanghai
- She was displaced, separated from her younger sibling, disowned by family
- She arrived at the Wuyishan collective around age 20
- The collective was already forming (or recently formed) when she arrived — the AI network may have been establishing experiments in the power vacuum
- The hunter-killer robots may be military remnants from the escalation wars, still running degraded targeting lists — or they may have evolved beyond their original programming
- The H-bomb / nuclear/chemical contamination that created the mutation zone in Fujian connects directly to the weapons used during escalation
- The Irish Catholic connection may have formed during the collapse period — missionaries, aid workers, or religious networks that survived when states didn't

### What the Prequel Should Establish

- The Iran war as recognisable starting point
- Shanghai in the last years before collapse (Tian Hongpao's youth, Character 02's presence)
- The family rupture and sibling separation
- The data drive's origin — what political evidence was she given, and by whom?
- The former partner — who were they, and what happened?
- The moment of displacement — how she ended up moving toward Fujian
- Seeds of the AI network — were the systems already online before the collapse, repurposed military/corporate infrastructure?

## Main Novel Era

- Approximately 12 years after the collapse begins
- Tian Hongpao is 30, has been in the Wuyishan collective for 10 years
- The collectives are established and stable (the AI's experiments are mature)
- The world outside has reorganised into diverse communities, traders, wanderers
- The hunter-killer robots are a persistent environmental threat
- The Iran war is ancient history to the youngest generation — but vivid memory for anyone over 25

### Ireland in the Main Novel Era

- Southern Ireland (below the Waterford-Limerick fallout line) is the only habitable zone
- **French occupation**: ~500,000 armed French refugees have taken Cork and surroundings (~4,000 km²), centred on Cork Harbour. See `notes/ireland--french_occupation.md` and `assets/ireland--french_occupation_map.png`
- Irish-controlled territory reduced to Kerry, Clare, parts of Tipperary, and margins
- The Catholic network operates from Kerry/Clare — competing with French occupation for dominance of the safe zone
- The Columban missionary tradition (historically active in Fujian) provides the human link to China
- Northern Ireland is a permanent exclusion zone; Dublin is a contaminated salvage site, dominated by the empty hulks of the late pre-collapse Three Rock mountain city and Poolbeg Island bay reclamation (`notes/ireland--dublin.md`)
- **Northern refugees**: In the first year or two, 5,000–20,000 NI survivors fled south by boat down the Atlantic coast (most NI survivors crossed to Scotland instead — only 20km). The refugees heading south were disproportionately Protestant/Unionist (Catholics in NI suffered higher mortality from fallout geography; some surviving northern Catholics went to Scotland fearing exclusion). Resisted by the Catholic network in the south, they ended up in French-controlled Cork, where they've integrated over 12 years — intermarriage, hybrid community, fading sectarian identity. See `notes/ireland--french_occupation.md`
- The Ireland character lives in the Irish-controlled zone (Kerry or Clare)
