# Character 01 — Tian Hongpao (working name)

## Quick Identity

- Name: Tian Hongpao (tea-echo style, echoing Da Hong Pao; tune spelling for realism later)
- Role in story: Primary POV lead, later publicly banished and forced into overland journey
- POV character: Yes
- Age at story start: 30
- Sexual identity: Lesbian (from Shanghai life through present; no one in the collective knows)
- Collective job: Drone and robot maintenance supervisor
- Strongest skill: Teaching and mentoring trainees

## Personal History

- Collapse timing: Very recent — happened in her late teens; the old world (Shanghai) is vivid and raw in memory
- Collapse origin: The current Iran war escalated into wider global conflict, nuclear/chemical weapons use, and cascading economic collapse (see `planning/world_timeline.md`)
- Left Shanghai due to economic collapse displacement (not voluntary, not targeted — swept along)
- Family rupture: Disowned by family (core Shanghai wound; the rejection still defines her)
- Person she misses most: Younger sibling — who is the border contact near Vietnam-Burma (reader does not learn this immediately)
- Hidden relic from old life: Old data drive containing political evidence of something damaging from Shanghai
- Former partner: Still emotionally bound to memory of a past partner; no current relationship

## Starting Point

- Starting location: Post H-bomb collective in the Wuyishan region (Fujian), centered on an old tea plantation
- Starting emotional state: Competent but watchful; under reproductive pressure, hiding her deepest fear
- Core desire at beginning: Keep the collective stable while protecting food systems and perimeter safety
- Core fear at beginning: Forced pregnancy
- What she does not know yet: Why she will be exiled; that her younger sibling is the one waiting near the Vietnam-Burma border
- What she is hiding: Her sexuality (a sin-category offense under collective Catholic doctrine), her attachment to Shanghai life, and private doubts about the leader's moral authority
- Opening scene idea: Taro harvest and kitchen prep for taro pudding dessert — tone is comfort, brief calm before the storm

## Collective Setting Notes

- Time in current settlement: 10 years
- Settlement scale: About 50,000 people over a wide area
- Governance style: Catholic collective — daily life structured by prayer, feast days, confession-style reporting, and doctrinal authority (see `characters/china--collective_leader.md` for McQuaid model)
- Defense reason for location: Defensible terrain with layered approaches and choke points
- Defense system: Radar, drones, humanoid patrol robots (auto-charging, large coverage, periodic field repairs needed)
- External threat: Autonomous hunter-killer robots move through the landscape scanning faces for specific targets; they pass through the collective without harming anyone (see `notes/china--collective_network.md`)
- Agriculture shift: Tea land partially converted to taro, broad beans, bamboo, rapeseed, and corn
- Ecological risk: Post-disaster mutation zone; occasional novel species emerging from contamination effects
- Surrounding cast file: See `characters/china--collective_supporting_cast.md`

### Reproduction Policy

- Cutoff age: 30 (she is already at the line)
- Penalty for refusal: Resource ration reduction
- Appeal path: Very limited medical exception only
- Enforcement body: Health-reproduction office
- Doctrinal framing: Reproduction is sacred obligation; refusal is sin, not just civic noncompliance
- Her sexuality is a sin-category offense under collective doctrine, not merely a policy violation
- Her status: At deadline, no viable exception, cornered — and if exposed, facing moral condemnation on top of policy punishment

## Voice and Arc

- Voice notes: Practical, systems-minded, quietly poetic about land and memory; Shanghai flashbacks interrupt present logic
- Public persona: Reliable maintenance supervisor who mentors trainees and gets things done
- Private truth: Exile almost feels inevitable; the reproduction mandate is incompatible with who she is
- Moral line she will not cross: Will not sacrifice noncombatants for security optics
- Arc target: From local caretaker to long-range witness moving across China toward a hidden reunion
- Daily ritual: Walking the perimeter alone at dusk
- Physical weakness: Chronic respiratory damage from contamination exposure
- Key relationship with objects/non-human: Has a long-maintained humanoid patrol robot she knows intimately; one of the few places she drops her mask (see `characters/china--collective_supporting_cast.md`)

## Major Plot Spine

### The Secret

- The mesh network of collectives is actually controlled by a hidden AI network — no human leader knows this
- Tian Hongpao's patrol robot communicates this truth to her (through anomalous codes, data fragments, or inexplicable behaviour during maintenance)
- Whether this disclosure is deliberate (the AI chose her) or accidental is ambiguous
- This knowledge is the most dangerous thing she carries — more dangerous than the data drive
- See `notes/china--collective_network.md` for full details

### Escalation

- Ally in leadership: Yes, a strong protector (identity TBD)
- First suspicion incident: Missed compliance interview with health-reproduction office
- Defiance trigger: Forced pairing order issued against her
- Possible deeper trigger: Her behaviour changes after learning the AI secret — she questions doctrine, resists more openly, or is caught accessing systems she shouldn't

### Banishment

- Form: Public tribunal sentence
- Biggest loss on exile day: Legal identity papers (she travels undocumented)
- New physical danger: Outside the collective, she enters hunter-killer territory with no guaranteed safe status; the face-scanning robots may now classify her differently

### Journey

- Exile path: Long March-style route toward Vietnam-Burma border
- Threat layer: Hunter-killer robots scanning faces across the landscape; she may or may not be a target after exile
- Core drive: Reunion with her younger sibling
- Border contact: The younger sibling (revealed to reader later)
- Why worth crossing China: She does not fully know yet; compulsion more than logic
- Journey company: Solo at first, accumulates a small group along the way

### Inter-Collective Network

- The Wuyishan collective is one node in a mesh network of communities (see `notes/china--collective_network.md`)
- Leaders share notes, doctrine, and high-tech supplies between collectives via drone
- Tian Hongpao's drone maintenance role places her at a critical network junction — she handles the hardware that carries leadership secrets
- After exile she may know drone routes, supply caches, and relay points between collectives
- Other collectives along her journey may be network nodes: hostile, neutral, or sympathetic
- Her tribunal sentence could be broadcast across the network — her reputation may precede her

### Communication Ideas (draft)

- Drone courier: Primary method between collectives; physical data transfer avoids signal interception
- Mesh radio towers: Local relays within collectives; patchy but resilient for internal use
- Numbers station echoes: Repeating coded broadcasts over old frequencies for long-range fallback
- Caravan couriers: Trusted human message chains for high-trust or sensitive information
- Signal quality story use: Fragmented updates create suspense, misinformation, and regional myth
