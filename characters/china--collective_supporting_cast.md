# Collective Supporting Cast (Early Scenes)

Use this file for characters who orbit Character 01 in the opening chapters.

## Youth Cohort (training-age group)

- Cohort summary: Mixed group entering mandatory labor-track training
- Age band in early scenes: Early teens
- Oldest in this specific cohort: 14
- Duty pathways: Agriculture, weapons drills, technical maintenance, and catechism-integrated schooling

## Trainers and Teachers

- Field trainer: Oversees agriculture tasks and yield targets
- Weapons instructor: Runs discipline, safety drills, and perimeter readiness
- Technical mentor: Trains drone/robot maintenance teams and radar troubleshooting
- School coordinator: Handles literacy, catechism, and collective-ethics curriculum drawn from the AI-generated texts

## Collective Leader

- Full profile: See `characters/china--collective_leader.md`
- Modelled on John Charles McQuaid's authority style fused with Maoist mass-mobilisation doctrine
- Uses AI to generate hybrid texts from McQuaid and Mao source material — these serve as the collective's living scripture
- Maintains external contact with Irish Catholic network (source of doctrinal framework and possibly outside resources)
- The collective operates as a Mao-Catholic way of life — prayer, self-criticism, feast days, confession-reporting, arranged pairings, sin-category offenses, denunciation sessions

## Humanoid Patrol Robot (minor character)

- One of the perimeter patrol robots, part of Tian Hongpao's maintenance responsibility
- Appears to be a standard patrol unit — not fully sentient, behavioural quirks from wear, patching, and environmental adaptation
- Tian Hongpao has maintained this unit for years; she knows its faults, rhythms, and workarounds intimately
- Communicates through status signals, movement patterns, and error codes she has learned to read
- Other people in the collective treat the robots as tools or threats; she treats this one with something closer to familiarity
- The robot's condition reflects the collective's health: when supplies are good it runs clean, when the network is stressed it degrades
- **Secret function:** This robot is an endpoint in the hidden AI network that actually controls the mesh of collectives (see `notes/china--collective_network.md`)
- It communicates the truth about the AI network to Tian Hongpao — possibly through anomalous codes, data fragments during maintenance, or behaviour that can only be explained by external coordination
- Whether this disclosure is deliberate (the AI chose her) or accidental (a maintenance exposure) is ambiguous
- Possible story functions:
  - Gives Tian Hongpao someone to be herself around (she talks to it while doing repairs, drops her public mask)
  - Creates a quiet loss at exile — she leaves it behind and no one else will maintain it the way she does
  - The channel through which the book's biggest secret reaches her
  - Could malfunction in a way that triggers or complicates her escalation toward the tribunal
  - Echoes the Tchaikovsky influence: non-human presence treated with genuine curiosity and something like empathy
- Name/designation: TBD (could be a serial number she has shortened into a nickname)

## Hidden System Rules (do not directly explain to reader early)

- Health-resource scarcity creates an unspoken age ceiling anxiety across the community
- Community leadership enforces life-stage deadlines through formal and informal penalties
- A punishment system exists, but exact logic and thresholds remain intentionally opaque

## Scene Utility Notes

- Show Catholic structure through texture, not exposition: grace before meals, saint-name duty rosters, confession queues, feast-day preparations
- Let children/teens repeat catechism phrases they only half understand to imply deeper indoctrination
- Use Tian Hongpao's observations to reveal stress fractures: who believes, who performs, who is afraid
- The collective should feel liveable and even warm in places — not a cartoon dystopia but a functioning community with a suffocating moral architecture underneath
