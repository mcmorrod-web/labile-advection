## Pre-Collapse Life

- Lived in **Xishuangbanna** (Jinghong, southern Yunnan) — not Shanghai like their older sibling
- Ran a **BnB / guesthouse** in the border region
- The BnB was a waystation for **telecom fraud operatives** — Chinese syndicates running scam compounds across the border in Myanmar's Shan State
- The sibling didn't run the fraud but profited from it: took the money, saw the people, didn't ask questions
- This gave them access to a network of smugglers, fixers, border runners, and people who move between China and Myanmar off the books
- They know the mountain paths, river crossings, and who controls each stretch of the border
- Moral position: complicit by proximity and profit, not by direct action — but the line between those blurs

## The Crossing to Tungapuri

- When the collapse hit, the sibling crossed from Xishuangbanna into Myanmar via the **Daluo border crossing** (~110km south of Jinghong, on the Daluo River)
- From Daluo, roughly **80km through mountainous eastern Shan State** to Kengtung/Kyaingtong — classical name **Tungapuri**
- Why Myanmar and not deeper into China: their survival network was on the Myanmar side. The fraud infrastructure (safe houses, radios, encrypted comms, supply caches, armed contacts) became survival infrastructure when the state collapsed.
- Tungapuri sits at 827m elevation — an old Shan princely city, founded by Tai Lue people, its own palace, a place with deep roots that outlasted every empire that tried to claim it

## What They Bring to the Story

- **A different world**: Tungapuri is not a collective. It's an old Southeast Asian crossroads city — Shan, Tai Lue, Chinese, Thai, Wa, and now whoever washed up during the collapse. A very different social and power structure from the Wuyishan collective.
- **Criminal-turned-survival networks**: The telecom fraud infrastructure is now the communication backbone of the Golden Triangle region. Radios, couriers, armed escorts, supply lines — all repurposed.
- **The reunion**: The emotional core of Tian Hongpao's entire journey ends here. What does it look like when two siblings who were separated by family rupture, collapse, and a decade of silence actually meet again? Are they still family, or strangers with shared blood?
- **Route knowledge**: If the story needs characters to move between China, Myanmar, Laos, and Thailand, this character knows how.
