## Pre-Collapse Background

- Senior CCP member being groomed for succession — on a path toward the highest levels of Party leadership
- Educated, politically sophisticated, trained in institutional control, factional management, and ideological discipline
- The collapse destroyed the state apparatus he was built to inherit
- He arrived in the Wuyishan region with political skills, organisational instincts, and a power vacuum to fill
- The CCP background explains everything about how he governs: the committee structures, the surveillance culture, the self-criticism sessions, the reproduction-as-civic-duty framing — these are Party reflexes dressed in new clothes
- The Irish Catholic connection and the AI-generated doctrine gave him a replacement ideology when the Party's was no longer viable
- He is not a convert in the spiritual sense — he is a political operator who recognised that Catholicism (fused with Maoist structure) could do what the CCP used to do: organise, motivate, and control a large population
- The tragedy: he was built for a country that no longer exists, and everything he does in the collective is an attempt to rebuild that scale of authority from scraps
- The irony: he thinks he is the architect; the AI network is actually running him as an experimental variable

## Key Influences: John Charles McQuaid + Mao Zedong (AI-synthesised)

The leadership uses AI to generate a hybrid doctrine by feeding in two source traditions:

- **McQuaid** — Catholic moral absolutism, institutional control over education/health/reproduction, sin-category social enforcement, confession as surveillance
- **Mao** — Mass-mobilisation rhetoric, self-criticism sessions, collective labor as spiritual purification, cult of correct thought, denunciation culture

The AI produces texts that fuse these traditions into a single governing scripture. Mao's language of revolutionary duty merges with Catholic moral obligation. Self-criticism sessions become indistinguishable from confession. Agricultural labor quotas are framed as both socialist duty and sacred stewardship. The resulting doctrine feels internally coherent — no one in the collective can easily trace which ideas came from which source.

### Why This Works as a Control Mechanism

- The texts have no single human author to challenge or discredit
- The AI can generate new texts on demand to address emerging situations (rulings, moral guidance, policy justifications)
- The hybrid language is unfamiliar enough that no one can appeal to an older "authentic" version
- Dissent requires arguing against a system that quotes both revolutionary sacrifice and divine will simultaneously
- The leader believes he controls the AI prompts and therefore controls doctrine — but presents the output as received truth
- **Hidden truth:** The AI network actually controls the mesh of collectives; the leader is an unwitting instrument (see `notes/china--collective_network.md`)

## Position and Power

- Governs ~50,000 people across a wide area of former tea plantation territory
- The collective operates as a Catholic way of life — not just policy but culture, ritual, daily structure
- Controls education, health, reproduction policy, resource allocation, tribunal sentencing, and defense
- Relationship to Tian Hongpao: Direct authority over her; presides over or sanctions her eventual public tribunal

### Inter-Collective Network

- The Wuyishan collective is one node in a mesh network of communities (see `notes/china--collective_network.md`)
- Leaders share notes, doctrinal texts, and directives between collectives via drone courier
- High-tech supplies (drone parts, robot components, medical equipment, AI hardware) are distributed across the network
- The AI-generated doctrine may be shared network-wide — giving the leader influence beyond their own collective
- Network governance is loose (alliance, not state) but doctrinal alignment creates soft cohesion

### How McQuaid's Model Maps to the Collective

| McQuaid's Ireland | Wuyishan Collective |
|---|---|
| Catholic education monopoly | All youth training filtered through doctrinal instruction |
| Mother and Child Scheme blocked | Health-reproduction office controls fertility as moral duty |
| Censorship board | Information access tightly controlled; outside signals filtered |
| Magdalene laundries / industrial schools | Punishment labor or reclassification for noncompliance |
| Parish informant networks | Community surveillance through duty-track supervisors and health officers |
| Archbishop as shadow state | Leader operates as both spiritual and civil authority |

### Cultural Layer in Daily Life (Mao-McQuaid hybrid)

- Morning and evening collective prayers structure the workday (liturgy meets political study session)
- Feast days blend saints' days with revolutionary commemorations
- Confession-style reporting sessions serve triple purpose: spiritual, political self-criticism, and surveillance
- Marriages and pairings are arranged or approved through the leader's office
- Reproduction is framed as sacred obligation and revolutionary duty simultaneously
- Homosexuality is both sin-category offense and counter-revolutionary deviation
- Children are taught catechism, Maoist-style collective ethics, and agricultural/weapons training as one unified curriculum
- Funerals and last rites carry genuine communal weight (not purely performative)
- Public denunciation sessions borrow from both struggle sessions and ecclesiastical tribunals
- The AI-generated texts are read aloud at gatherings — treated as living scripture that can be updated

## Irish Catholic Connection

- Maintains active contact with an Irish Catholic group or network
- Historical precedent: Columban missionaries operated in Fujian from the 1920s onward
- The connection is the source of the Catholic framework itself — doctrine, liturgical structure, institutional design
- May also provide practical benefits: outside information, medical knowledge, coded communications
- The leader was not spiritually converted — he recognised the Catholic institutional model as a viable governance framework when the CCP structure collapsed
- His CCP training made him uniquely equipped to see how McQuaid's Ireland worked: a parallel state operating through moral authority, education control, and informant networks — exactly the tools a Party cadre understands
- The Irish connection may have formed during the collapse period — missionaries or aid workers who survived and found a powerful, organised Chinese official willing to adopt their framework in exchange for external support
- A character based in southern Ireland (below the Waterford-Limerick fallout line) is connected to this network — see `characters/ireland--character.md`
