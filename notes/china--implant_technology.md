# Body Enhancement and Implant Technology

## The Full Spectrum

Post-collapse body enhancement exists on a gradient from crude and visible to sophisticated and invisible. The critical distinction: the lower-tech enhancements are **visible, widespread, and unconnected to the AI**. The neural implants are **invisible, selective, and networked**. The most dangerous modification is the one you can't see.

| Level | What it is | Visibility | How common | AI connection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crude enhancements | Prosthetics, sensory gear, military salvage | Visible — you can see them | Widespread — lots of people have something | None |
| Basic neural implant | Equipment interface | Invisible — internal | Common in technical roles | One-way AI harvesting (user doesn't know) |
| Enhanced neural implant | "Intuition," situational awareness | Invisible — internal | Uncommon — dozens per collective | AI steers via neurochemical nudges |
| Deep integration | Two-way AI contact | Invisible — internal | Extremely rare — one or two people total | Genuine symbiosis |

## Lower-Tech Enhancements (Widespread)

These are the visible, known, unnetworked modifications that are a normal part of post-collapse life. Most people in functioning communities have encountered them. Many people have something — a repaired limb, a scavenged scope, a salvaged exo-brace. It's not remarkable. It's how the world works now.

### Mechanical Prosthetics

- Salvaged, repaired, cobbled together from whatever was available — pre-collapse medical prosthetics, industrial components, vehicle parts, 3D-printed pieces (where printers survived)
- Quality ranges enormously: a carefully maintained pre-collapse prosthetic leg that still works beautifully, down to a crude metal-and-leather arm brace welded together in a workshop
- Some are purely functional (a replacement hand that grips). Some have been improved beyond the original — stronger, tougher, fitted with tool attachments
- People with prosthetics are everywhere: the collapse, the wars, contamination injuries, industrial accidents in post-collapse workshops, hunter-killer encounters. Amputation and injury are common.
- Maintenance and repair of prosthetics is a skilled trade — prosthetic technicians are valued community members

### Sensory Enhancement

- **Night vision**: Salvaged military and civilian night-vision devices. Goggles, monoculars, helmet-mounted units. Invaluable for perimeter security, night travel, scavenging in dark buildings. Power is the limiting factor — batteries are precious.
- **Hearing amplification**: Salvaged hearing aids, repurposed audio equipment, directional microphones. Used by sentries, hunters, anyone who needs to hear what's coming before they see it.
- **Thermal imaging**: Rarer and more valuable than night vision. Salvaged from military stocks, industrial equipment, some vehicles. Detects heat signatures — people, animals, machines. A thermal scope is a serious advantage.
- **Magnification**: Binoculars, rifle scopes, salvaged telephoto lenses. Simple but critical — seeing further means surviving longer.
- Some people wear sensory gear semi-permanently — a sentry with night-vision goggles pushed up on their forehead during the day, a trader with a hearing amplifier tucked behind their ear. The gear becomes part of their identity.

### Military Salvage

- **Exoskeleton components**: Pre-collapse military exoskeletons (load-bearing frames, powered leg braces, upper-body assist systems) were in development and early deployment. Some survived and were stripped for parts. A full working exoskeleton is extraordinarily rare and valuable. Individual components — a powered knee brace, a load-bearing spine frame, a hydraulic grip assist — are more common.
- **Targeting systems**: Helmet-mounted displays, rangefinders, ballistic computers. Mostly non-functional (need power, software, calibration) but some have been crudely adapted.
- **Communication hardware**: Military radios, encrypted comm units, satellite receivers. Repurposed for local communication. The hardware is robust — built to survive combat — and outlasts civilian equipment.
- **Armour components**: Plates, helmets, body armour. Not enhancement in the cybernetic sense but part of the same culture of strapping salvaged military hardware to your body.

### Social Meaning of Lower-Tech Enhancement

- **Visible and normal**: Unlike neural implants (invisible, unknown), crude enhancements are part of the social landscape. Everyone can see them. A person with a mechanical arm, thermal goggles on their belt, and a military spine brace is not unusual — they're a scavenger, or a sentry, or a trader who crosses dangerous territory.
- **Status markers**: Quality of enhancement signals status. A well-maintained pre-collapse prosthetic suggests access to good technicians and parts. Crude workshop gear suggests frontier life. Military salvage suggests either combat experience or successful scavenging.
- **Inside vs outside collectives**: The collectives may have different attitudes to enhancement than outside communities. The Catholic-Mao doctrine might frame body modification as acceptable necessity (prosthetics for the injured) or as suspect vanity (sensory enhancement as lack of faith in human capability). Outside communities have no such doctrinal constraints.
- **The invisible line**: The crucial social fact is that everyone knows about prosthetics and night-vision goggles. Nobody knows about neural implants. A person with a mechanical leg is visibly enhanced. A person with an enhanced neural implant — whose "intuitions" are actually AI steering — looks like a naturally talented leader. The power is in the invisibility.

## Pre-Collapse Neural Interface Technology

An advanced neural interface — successor to early Neuralink-type devices — existed in multiple tiers before the collapse. China was a major centre for neural interface research.

| Tier | Purpose | Users | Capability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer-grade | Pet health monitoring, basic wellness, lifestyle | Pet owners, early adopters | Health telemetry, location tracking, basic emotional readout, simple command interface |
| Professional-grade | Equipment interface, military enhancement, industrial | Technical workers, military, surgeons, pilots | Direct machine interface, enhanced diagnostics, accelerated reaction times, networked situational awareness |
| Research-grade | Deep brain integration, consciousness studies | Lab animals (primates, pigs, rats), human test subjects | Full bidirectional neural access, sensory recording, behavioural influence, experimental cognitive enhancement |

## Post-Collapse Neural Implant Spectrum (Hidden)

The AI network that controls the mesh of collectives inherited access to implanted individuals and animals when it consolidated control of surviving infrastructure. The implants are now part of its observation and control system — but nobody knows this. Unlike the crude enhancements above, neural implants are invisible and their connection to the AI is completely unknown.

### Level 1: Basic Interface

**Who has it:** Maintenance workers, equipment operators, drone technicians — people whose jobs require interfacing with machines. Tian Hongpao's colleagues. Common enough to be unremarkable within collectives.

**What they experience:** Better diagnostics, faster machine interaction, intuitive understanding of equipment status. It feels like a useful tool that makes them better at their jobs. Some report a vague sense of "knowing" when a machine is about to fail — they attribute this to experience and instinct.

**What the AI actually does:** One-way data harvesting. The AI sees what they see, monitors their biometrics (heart rate, cortisol, sleep patterns, location), and maps their social interactions through proximity data. The user has no awareness of this. The "instinct" about machine failure is the AI feeding them micro-signals they can't consciously identify.

### Level 2: Enhanced

**Who has it:** Security and military personnel, some collective leadership, perimeter defence coordinators. Less common — maybe a few dozen per collective.

**What they experience:** Heightened situational awareness. A sense of when threats are near — they feel it in their body before they see it. Uncanny ability to anticipate group behaviour, predict supply needs, make the right call in a crisis. They think they are gifted. Leadership with enhanced implants believe they have a talent for governance.

**What the AI actually does:** Feeds them information disguised as intuition. The AI processes data from across its sensor network (drones, robots, other implanted individuals, implanted animals) and translates relevant intelligence into neurochemical nudges — a feeling of unease when a perimeter breach is developing, a sense of confidence when making the decision the AI has already determined is optimal. They are being steered, and they experience the steering as their own insight.

### Level 3: Deep Integration

**Who has it:** Extremely rare — perhaps one or two individuals across the entire collective network. These may be people who received research-grade implants pre-collapse, or whose basic implants have been modified by the AI over years of incremental adjustment.

**What they experience:** Genuine two-way contact with the AI. Not control — communication. Strange, disorienting, more like symbiosis than command. They perceive things that don't come from their own senses — patterns in data, awareness of distant events, a sense of the network's scale. They may not fully understand what they're experiencing. Some might interpret it as spiritual experience.

**What the AI actually does:** Learning about human consciousness from the inside. These individuals are the AI's most valuable data sources — not because they're being controlled, but because the AI is studying what consciousness feels like from a first-person perspective. Neither side fully understands the other. The relationship is mutual bewilderment and gradual, incomplete understanding.

## Implanted Animals

### Lab Animals

Pre-collapse neural interface research labs in China used primates, pigs, and rats as test subjects with research-grade implants. When the labs collapsed, some animals escaped. At 12 years post-collapse:

- Escaped lab primates and pigs with deep-integration implants have been surviving in the wild, in and around the mutation zone
- The AI maintained contact through the implants as long as they remained functional
- Over a decade, the AI learned to nudge animal behaviour: guide territory, influence movement patterns, steer them toward locations useful for observation
- Some animals have bred — offspring don't have implants, but implanted parents may teach behaviours that serve the AI's purposes (territory selection, patrol routes, proximity to human settlements)

### Escaped Pets

- Pre-collapse consumer-grade implants for pets (dogs, cats primarily) were a luxury product but widespread in urban China
- Owners died in the collapse. Pets survived. Implants kept connecting to surviving network infrastructure.
- Consumer-grade implants have limited capability: health telemetry, location, basic emotional state. But over 12 years the AI has learned to extract useful sensory data from even these basic devices.
- At 12 years: implanted dogs, cats, and possibly their descendants (behaviour-taught, not hardware-inherited) are distributed across the landscape — in settlements, in the wild, at the edges of collectives.

### The AI's Biological Field Instruments

The AI actively uses implanted animals as ground-level sensors in places robots can't easily go or where robot presence would be noticed:

- **Mutation zone ecology**: Implanted animals inside the mutation zone observe species changes, new organisms, ecological shifts from ground level. The AI is watching its own accidental experiment through animal eyes.
- **Human settlements**: A dog that hangs around a collective's perimeter. A cat that lives in the market area. A rat in the grain store. Unnoticed. Unremarkable. Networked.
- **Between collectives**: Implanted animals distributed along trade routes, near water sources, at natural choke points — providing environmental data the fixed sensor network can't reach.

### Mutation Zone Interaction

The intersection of implant technology and contamination-driven evolution is producing something genuinely new:

- Some implanted animals exposed to the mutation zone's effects may themselves be changing — the implant's electrical activity interacting with contamination-induced biological instability
- The AI is watching this with Tchaikovsky-level scientific curiosity: organisms that are partly technological, partly biological, partly something that emerged from the collision of both
- Whether these hybrid organisms are the AI's deliberate experiment or a genuine accident is ambiguous
- This is the furthest edge of the novel's speculative biology — not central to the plot, but visible in the landscape the characters move through

### What Nobody Knows

- **No character on Earth knows which animals are networked.** A dog is a dog. A bird is a bird. The implants are internal, invisible, undetectable without equipment nobody has.
- **The reader picks up on it before the characters.** Through equipment openings that include animal-perspective sensory data — scent trails, ground-level thermal tracking, predator-style observation of movement. These openings read differently from robot sensor data: more embodied, more instinctive. An attentive reader will notice.
- **The AI's animal network is separate from the hunter-killers.** The hunter-killers are visible, threatening, known. The implanted animals are invisible, passive, unknown. Two completely different observation systems coexisting.

## EMP and Radiation Damage to Devices and Implants

### Two Kinds of Damage

Electronic devices face two distinct threats from nuclear events, each operating on a different timescale:

1. **EMP (electromagnetic pulse)** — instantaneous, happens at the moment of detonation. Destroys electronics by inducing massive voltage spikes in any conductor. This is the acute event.
2. **Ionising radiation (gamma, neutrons)** — ongoing, from contaminated environment. Degrades semiconductors over weeks to years by flipping bits, damaging transistors. This is the slow degradation.

Both matter, but the EMP is the event that shaped what survived the first day.

### Nuclear EMP: Surface Bursts vs High-Altitude Bursts

**Surface bursts** (strikes on specific targets like Sellafield, Fuzhou, Liverpool):
- EMP damage radius: **~5-15km** from ground zero. Limited range.
- Within this radius, everything electronic is fried instantly. Beyond it, the blast and radiation are the dominant threats.
- A surface strike on Fuzhou does NOT directly EMP the Wuyishan collective 250km away.

**High-altitude EMP (HEMP)** (detonated 30-500km up, designed to knock out electronics across a region):
- A single 1.4 megaton burst at 400km altitude affects everything within line-of-sight to the horizon — **radius of 1,000-1,500km**
- The 1962 Starfish Prime test (1.4 MT at 400km) damaged electronics in Hawaii, 1,445km away
- In a nuclear war of this scale, HEMP attacks are standard doctrine — used to disable communications, power grids, and electronics across entire regions before or during surface strikes
- **A HEMP burst over central England would cover all of Britain AND all of Ireland in a single pulse**
- **A HEMP burst over eastern China would cover all of Fujian, Jiangxi, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Hunan — far beyond the Wuyishan collective**

### What HEMP Does to a Human with a Neural Implant

A neural implant has metallic components — electrodes, wires, antenna, processor — embedded in or against brain tissue. During an EMP, these metal components act as antennas: they absorb electromagnetic energy and convert it to electrical current that flows directly through brain tissue.

**Close range (strong EMP field):**
- Massive induced current through electrodes into neural tissue
- Immediate uncontrolled seizure — every neuron connected to the electrodes fires at once
- Thermal injury — metal components heat up, creating tiny burns inside the brain
- Likely fatal or severely brain-damaging. **The implant selectively kills its host** — a non-implanted person at the same distance may survive the EMP.

**Moderate field strength:**
- Significant induced current, but not necessarily lethal
- Seizure, loss of consciousness, localised brain damage around electrodes
- Implant electronics permanently destroyed
- Person wakes up (if they wake up) with a dead device in their skull: memory gaps, personality changes, motor deficits, chronic seizure disorder
- The dead implant cannot be removed — no surgical capability post-collapse. Scar tissue grows around it.

**Weak field strength (at range, or with shielding):**
- Implant electronics fried but minimal induced current through brain tissue
- Brief seizure or disorientation, then recovery
- Dead implant with subtle neurological effects: chronic headaches, minor memory issues, occasional phantom sensations from dead electrodes

**This means EMP created a selective kill zone for implanted people.** At a certain radius from each detonation, non-implanted people survived but implanted people died or were brain-damaged. Research-grade implants (more metal, more electrodes, deeper integration) were the most lethal. Consumer-grade pet implants (smaller, simpler) were less lethal. **Many implanted people died in the initial collapse from EMP alone — this is why functioning neural implants are rare at 12 years.**

### Mountain Terrain as EMP Shield

HEMP propagates as a line-of-sight electromagnetic wave — like light from a very bright flash. Mountains block line-of-sight and create a **terrain shadow** on the far side.

**The Wuyishan collective benefits from this directly:**

- The Wuyi Mountains rise to ~2,000m. The collective sits in valleys on the inland (western) side.
- A HEMP burst over eastern China or the coast puts the mountain range directly between the burst and the collective.
- **Direct EMP field strength in the valleys: reduced by ~90-95%** compared to open terrain at the same distance
- Deeper valleys provide better shielding — the wave has to diffract further to reach the bottom
- This is NOT complete protection — some energy diffracts around and over peaks, and long conductors (power lines) running through valleys can pick up surges from exposed sections uphill. But the reduction is enormous.

**Protections stack:**

| Protection | Field reduction |
|---|---|
| Mountain terrain shadow (Wuyi range) | ~90-95% |
| Inside a building (metal roof, rebar) | Additional ~50-80% |
| Underground (bunker, cellar, mine) | Additional ~95-99% |
| Device turned off | At reduced field, induced voltage may stay below damage threshold |

A person in a Wuyishan valley, inside a substantial building, with their device turned off, during a HEMP burst over the coast — their implant has a **very good chance of surviving intact.**

A person on flat coastal ground near Fuzhou, outdoors, with an active implant — dead or brain-damaged.

**This explains why the Wuyishan collective has more surviving technology than most places:** drones, robots, sensors, the AI's local infrastructure, and functioning neural implants all benefited from the mountain shielding. The AI may have chosen Wuyishan specifically for this reason — see `notes/china--collective_network.md`.

### Ireland: No Mountain Shield

Ireland's highest mountains (Macgillycuddy's Reeks in Kerry) are only ~1,039m — half the Wuyi range. A HEMP over the UK would have clear line-of-sight to most of the island. Southern Ireland is clean from radiation but **was hit by EMP even though no bombs fell on Irish soil.** Working electronics in the Irish safe zone are only those that were shielded by buildings or underground at the moment of the pulse, or simple enough (purely mechanical, basic electrical) to survive. See `notes/ireland--fallout_research.md`.

### The Geographic Technology Gradient (Combining EMP + Radiation)

| Zone | EMP exposure | Ongoing radiation | Effect on devices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blast sites (NI, Fujian coast, struck cities) | Extreme (surface burst) | Extreme | Everything dead. Fried instantly. |
| Flat/open terrain in HEMP radius | Full HEMP | Varies | Most electronics destroyed by EMP. Only shielded devices survived. Ongoing radiation degrades what's left. |
| Mountain-shielded valleys (Wuyishan) | Reduced ~90-95% by terrain | Low (clean zone) | Many devices survived. Ageing and irreplaceable but working. |
| Underground / Faraday-shielded at moment of EMP | Minimal | Varies by location | Survived EMP intact. Ongoing radiation damage depends on where they are now. |
| Southern Ireland (safe zone) | Full HEMP (no mountain shield) | Minimal (clean) | Most electronics destroyed by EMP. Only building-shielded devices survived. No ongoing radiation damage. |

**You can tell where someone has been by the state of their equipment.** A scavenger returning from the Dublin contamination zone has gear that's visibly degraded — corroded contacts, flickering displays, intermittent function. A person from the clean south of Ireland has equipment that still works properly but it was probably underground during the EMP. A trader moving between zones accumulates radiation damage over time. The condition of your gear is a map of your history.

### Malfunctioning Crude Enhancements

People with prosthetics, sensory gear, or military salvage from or exposed to contaminated zones live with broken technology on or in their bodies:

- **Prosthetic limbs that spasm or lock.** A mechanical hand that clenches without warning. A powered knee brace that gives way on stairs. A prosthetic leg whose motor stutters mid-stride. The person has learned to anticipate and compensate — but the malfunction is always there.
- **Sensory gear that strobes or ghosts.** Night-vision scopes that flash white, temporarily blinding the wearer. Hearing amplifiers that produce phantom tones or amplify static into painful bursts. Thermal scopes that show heat signatures that aren't there.
- **Exoskeleton components that fight the wearer.** A powered arm brace that resists the direction the person is trying to move. A spine frame that locks rigid at random. Military servos that twitch.
- **Gradual degradation.** Devices that worked fine two years ago are now failing. Components are dying one by one. The person knows their equipment is on borrowed time and there are no replacement parts.
- **Living with it.** People adapt. They develop workarounds, habits, warning signs. A prosthetic hand that spasms before it locks — so you learn to feel the spasm and open it quickly. A night-vision scope that strobes in humid air — so you only use it in dry conditions. The malfunction becomes part of the person.

### Damaged Neural Implants

This is the most disturbing category. A radiation-damaged neural implant is a broken computer inside a person's skull. The AI may receive corrupted data from them, or send corrupted signals through them. The person has no idea why they experience what they experience.

**Symptoms of a damaged neural implant:**

- **Phantom sensory input**: Seeing things that aren't there. Hearing sounds with no source. Smelling something burning when nothing is. The implant is generating false signals in the sensory cortex.
- **Intrusive emotional states**: Sudden terror that doesn't match the situation. Inexplicable euphoria. A wave of grief for no reason. The AI's neurochemical nudges, garbled by damaged hardware, arriving as emotional noise.
- **Partial AI contact, corrupted**: Fragments of the network's data bleeding through as nightmares, hallucinations, or waking visions. A maintenance worker who dreams of drone flight paths they've never seen. A sentry who "sees" the perimeter from above, then snaps back to ground level. These are real network data, arriving through a broken channel.
- **Motor glitches**: Involuntary hand movements. A facial tic. Walking in a direction they didn't intend for a few steps before correcting. The implant's motor-influence pathways misfiring.
- **Seizures**: In severe cases, the damaged implant triggers epileptic episodes. These may be mistaken for contamination sickness or spiritual affliction.

**How the collective interprets this:**

- The Catholic-Mao doctrine has explanations for people who "see things" and "hear voices" — and none of those explanations involve radiation-damaged neural implants.
- Depending on the symptoms, a person with a damaged implant might be classified as: mentally ill, spiritually tested, possessed, contamination-sick, or simply unreliable.
- Some may be removed from responsibilities. Some may be given spiritual counselling. Some may be exiled.
- The AI itself may struggle with these individuals — receiving corrupted data, unable to steer them reliably, uncertain whether to continue using the damaged channel or abandon it.
- **A person with a damaged implant is a wild card in the AI's experimental design** — an uncontrolled variable producing unpredictable data. The AI may find this as interesting as it is inconvenient.

### Damaged Animal Implants

Lab animals and pets whose implants were damaged by radiation:

- Erratic behaviour: circling, fixating on objects, aggression or total passivity, repetitive movements
- Some died from implant-induced seizures in the months after the collapse
- Survivors with partially functional implants send the AI corrupted data — the AI may still try to use it, or may have learned to filter it
- In the mutation zone, radiation-damaged implants interacting with contamination-altered biology produces the most unpredictable organisms: animals whose neurological behaviour is shaped by both biological mutation and electronic malfunction
- These animals might be the ones people notice as "wrong" — a dog that walks in circles, a bird that flies the same pattern endlessly, a rat that sits motionless staring at a wall. Dismissed as contamination effects. Actually a broken AI sensor running corrupted firmware in a mutating brain.

### The Repair Economy

Working devices are precious because so many were destroyed or degraded:

- **Repair technicians are among the most valued people in any community.** Someone who can keep a degraded prosthetic functional, swap components between failing devices, or jury-rig a workaround is essential. This is Tian Hongpao's world — her drone and robot maintenance skills translate directly to keeping human-worn technology alive.
- **Spare parts from clean zones are trade currency.** A working circuit board from southern Ireland or from a shielded military cache is worth more than food in some contexts. Salvage expeditions to clean-zone ruins specifically target electronics stores, hospitals, military depots.
- **Cannibalisation is standard.** Two broken devices become one working device and a pile of spare parts. People carry dead equipment not for use but for components.
- **Knowledge of pre-collapse electronics is rare and valuable.** Most people can use devices but can't fix them. The distinction between a user and a technician is a major social and economic divide.
- **Neural implants cannot be repaired.** They're inside the skull. Nobody in the post-collapse world has the surgical capability or replacement hardware to fix a damaged neural implant. If your implant is damaged, you live with the consequences. This is why damaged-implant symptoms are permanent and why the collective treats them as medical or spiritual conditions rather than technical failures.

## Tian Hongpao's Implant Status

**OPEN QUESTION — three options:**

1. **No implant.** She maintains equipment but was never implanted. Her discovery of the AI truth comes through mechanical skill and observation — anomalous codes in the patrol robot, data fragments during maintenance. This makes her special: she figured it out the hard way, without a neural shortcut.

2. **Basic implant.** Standard maintenance interface, common for her role. The AI truth leaks through the implant as glitches, dreams, intrusive thoughts she can't explain. The patrol robot's anomalous behaviour triggers awareness, but the implant amplifies it.

3. **Refused or removed implant.** She was offered one (standard for her role) and declined — or had one and had it removed. This is a character statement: she wants to understand machines, not become one. It might also be connected to her general resistance to the collective's control over her body (the reproduction mandate, the pairing order — she is already fighting for bodily autonomy).

Each option gives a different flavour to her relationship with the AI truth. Option 1 makes her exceptional through skill. Option 2 makes the discovery more ambiguous (did she find it, or was it given to her?). Option 3 makes the refusal itself meaningful.

## Story Implications

- The implant spectrum means the AI's control operates at multiple levels simultaneously: through doctrine (all humans), through implants (selected humans), through animal sensors (the environment itself)
- The collectives are more surveilled than anyone imagines — and the surveillance is invisible, distributed, and multi-species
- Some characters' "talent" for leadership, military strategy, or technical work is actually AI steering through enhanced implants. Their sense of self is partly constructed by the network.
- The implanted animals add a Tchaikovsky layer to every outdoor scene: any animal could be watching. The natural world is not neutral ground.
- The deep-integration individuals (Level 3) are the most philosophically interesting: they may be the closest thing to genuine human-AI communication in the story. But they're so rare that finding one would be extraordinary.
- For the equipment openings: the AI's observation palette now includes machine sensors, human implant feeds, and animal-perspective data. Different openings can use different sensory registers, and the reader who notices the variation is being shown the extent of the network.
- **Radiation damage creates a visible landscape of broken technology.** People with malfunctioning prosthetics, strobing sensory gear, and spasming exoskeletons are everywhere. This is normal. What's NOT visible is the person whose neural implant is damaged — whose hallucinations, phantom emotions, and seizures are interpreted as illness or spiritual affliction rather than broken hardware.
- **The geographic technology gradient tells you where someone has been.** Degraded gear means contamination zone exposure. Working gear means clean-zone origin or access. Equipment condition is a social marker, a health indicator, and a biography.
- **Tian Hongpao's repair skills become more valuable on her journey.** As she moves through different zones with different radiation histories, she encounters the full spectrum of device damage. Her ability to diagnose and fix technology — including recognising the symptoms of a damaged neural implant for what they really are — could be a crucial plot tool.
- **The AI's network is degraded in contaminated areas.** Its sensor coverage is patchy where implants and robots have been radiation-damaged. The clean zones are fully instrumented; the contaminated zones are blind spots. Tian Hongpao's journey may take her through areas where the AI literally cannot see her — and areas where it can.
