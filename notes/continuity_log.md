## Structural Device: Equipment Openings

- Every chapter begins with a short passage from the equipment/AI network perspective
- Early chapters: reads as ambient landscape/weather — reader doesn't know what's speaking
- Middle chapters: patterns emerge — the equipment tracks specific people, anticipates behaviour
- Late chapters (post-reveal): the reader understands they've been reading the AI's surveillance all along
- Full rules in `planning/tone_and_style.md`

## Check Before Finalizing a Chapter

- Ages and dates still match
- Injuries and physical details stay consistent
- Knowledge is POV-accurate (no accidental omniscience)
- Character voice remains distinct
- Equipment opening is consistent with what the AI could actually observe at this point in the story
- Equipment voice does not use human moral categories (no judgment, empathy, or cruelty — just attention)
